VAWES / Material study

Apple-inspired Liquid Glass

A web translation of Apple’s 2025 material: refraction instead of simple blur, a vertical specular highlight, adaptive tint, floating controls, and less chrome around the content. Move the pointer over the mockup — the highlight follows.
